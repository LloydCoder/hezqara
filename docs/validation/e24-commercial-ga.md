# E24 — Commercialization, Independent Validation & GA

Status: IMPLEMENTED — awaiting final forensic audit.

E24 provides evidence-backed commercial plan metadata and explicit launch gates. A launch gate is blocked unless its required evidence exists; pricing is stored in minor currency units and does not hard-code a final market price.

Final GA requires all prior phase CI/security/E2E/DR/interoperability evidence plus independent validation where applicable. Production deployment to Vercel is intentionally deferred until the final forensic audit passes.
